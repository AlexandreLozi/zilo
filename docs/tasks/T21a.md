**Slices, one branch `t21a-sdk-jobs`, commit per slice:**

**Slice 1: jobs and events**
1. Jobs client on top of the existing T13 transport, auth and credentials (no second HTTP stack):
   create (`POST /jobs`), get, list, cancel (with and without `discardPages`), dispose (`DELETE`).
   - Per-job family selection and deterministic rollover (R-A10, R-A15, R-A16): a job stays on
     the family that created it. A 404 from another family is "not found", never retried on a
     different family.
   - Effective settings and adjustments (R-J5, R-J6) are surfaced unchanged and typed.
   - State machine per §5.2. The SDK never invents a transition the agent did not send, except
     the SDK-local `agent-restarted` from T13's lost-session (§12, R-R1 to R-R3).
2. Events over fetch streaming (R-E1; no `EventSource`):
   - an SSE parser that handles chunk splits at any byte, CRLF and LF, comments and multi-line
     framing per R-E2;
   - two-step decoding (R-V4): envelope first, then the known `JobEvent`; unknown types are
     ignored after their `seq` is recorded;
   - `seq` tracking and resume from the last *processed* `seq` (R-E4, R-E5) on reconnect, with
     bounded backoff and abort;
   - `done` closes the stream; a `fatal` error is followed by `failed` and then `done` (R-E3);
   - R-E6 to R-E8 as written.
   - Public API: an async-iterable or subscriber, plus a promise for the terminal `done`. Every
     listener and timer is released on abort, dispose and `done`; prove no leak over 20 cycles.

**Slice 2: pages and export**
3. Pages (§7, R-P1 to R-P4): list and fetch, verify `Content-Length` (R-X3), typed errors.
4. Export (§8, R-X1 to R-X7): formats per the contract, retry where the contract allows it.
   - **Settled receive-then-DELETE (R-X5, R-X7):** receive and verify every body, and only then
     `DELETE`. **Never send `deleteAfterExport`.**
   - The export-and-delete-without-stop refusal, plus the stop, cancel-keep-pages and late-page
     report vector (CONTRACT §8, "Acceptance vector").
   - `413 limits-exceeded` and the other limits (R-X6, §10) map to typed errors.
