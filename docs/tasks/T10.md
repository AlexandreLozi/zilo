**Target:** `api/openapi.yaml` (OpenAPI 3.1) and `api/CONTRACT.md`. Together they are the normative
wire contract between the Zilo agent (GPT lane) and `@zilo/sdk` (FE lane).

**Read first:**

  (unreviewed)`, and list those rules in an "Unreviewed inputs" section of CONTRACT.md.

**Must specify:**
1. **Versioning and compatibility:**
   - `/zilo/v1` prefix and `hello` (product, version, protocol, os, arch, capabilities[]).
   - A breaking change means `/zilo/v2`. Additive changes are gated by capabilities.
   - Clients must ignore unknown fields and unknown event types.
2. **Transport rules:**
   - Host allowlist, and the 403 `host-denied` response.
   - Exact-Origin CORS: reflect the origin plus `Vary: Origin`. CORS headers also go on errors.
   - `OPTIONS` preflight is served without auth: 204, with allowed methods and headers.
   - No PNA headers. `Cache-Control: no-store` on sensitive responses.
   - No state-changing GETs.
3. **Auth:**
   - Consent request flow for unknown origins: which endpoint triggers the native prompt, the
     **consent-pending** signal and polling behaviour, rate limit and cool-down after a denial.
   - Token issuance (bound to origin, random, with expiry), renewal, revocation, and the
     `token-revoked`/`unauthorized-origin` errors.
   - Installer-baked origins still authenticate (D-004).
4. **Devices:** list, refresh, caps (sources flatbed/feeder/duplex, DPI list or range, colour modes,
   page sizes, limits), and the driver enum including `virtual`.
5. **Jobs:**
   - Ownership by origin and token. Cross-origin access returns 404 (do not leak existence).
   - The state machine `queued → scanning → completed | failed | cancelled → disposed`.
   - Cancel versus dispose.
   - Concurrency limits (one active scan per device, N jobs per origin).
   - Capability validation, and the reported **effective settings**.
6. **Events (SSE over fetch):**
   - Event types and schemas: `progress`, `page`, `error`, `state`, `done`.
   - A monotonic `seq` and `Last-Event-ID` reconnect, with a replay window. Heartbeat comments.
7. **Pages:**
   - 0-based stable numbering, with duplex side marked.
   - Formats and the `maxWidth` thumbnail parameter. `ETag` and caching rules.
8. **Export:**
   - PDF, TIFF, JPEG and PNG (images may come back as a zip or one page per request; choose and
     justify).
   - A page selection with per-page order and rotation, and quality.
   - Retry and multiple exports. `deleteAfterExport` is opt-in.
   - Size limits.
9. **Errors:** RFC 9457 problem+json, with stable `code` values. Include at least every code in T05
   `copy.md` (`device-busy`, `feeder-empty`, `paper-jam`, `cover-open`, `timeout`, `disconnected`,
   `permission`, `unauthorized-origin`, `token-revoked`, `export-failed`, `limits-exceeded`,
   `unknown`) plus `host-denied`, `consent-pending`, `consent-denied`, `not-found`, `invalid-settings`
   and `unsupported-capability`. Give the HTTP status and retryability of each.
10. **Limits and retention:** pages, pixels, bytes, duration, concurrency, TTL, user-private temp.
11. **Safari path:** only a placeholder section. S1 and S2 are unapproved (D-003); document the
    capability flag the SDK reads.
